# Company Code of Conduct & Workplace Standards

## Core Values
1. **Psychological Safety & Respect**: We foster an inclusive environment where questions are celebrated and failure is treated as a learning opportunity.
2. **Customer & Team Obsession**: Deliver high-reliability services that simplify work for teammates and external customers.
3. **Radical Candor with Empathy**: Direct, constructive feedback delivered with genuine care.

## Working Hours & Flexibility
- **Core Hours**: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM in your local time zone for synchronous meetings and team alignment.
- **Flexible Bands**: Remaining hours can be structured flexibly around personal productivity and time zone needs.

## Weekly Timesheet Policy
- All hourly and salaried employees must review and finalize their weekly timesheet by **Friday at 5:00 PM local time**.
- Timesheets ensure timely payroll processing, project cost tracking, and compliance.
- Direct questions to People Operations at `people-ops@company.com`.
